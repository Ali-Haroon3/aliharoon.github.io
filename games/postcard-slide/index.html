Postcard Slide

A sliding picture puzzle for iPhone. Move strips of paper, turn four-piece hinges, and rotate the border to restore illustrated travel postcards.

What is the iPhone puzzle where one swipe fixes a lighthouse?

The picture is Cape Ember, the first puzzle in Postcard Slide. Slide its middle strip one place to the right to line up the lighthouse.

How do I rotate four tiles together?

Tap a four-square hinge diagram below the picture. Swipe around the center of those four pieces or tap a rotation arrow. The outer frame moves eight pieces around the center square.

How many postcards can I collect?

The album holds 24 original illustrations. Each has three campaign arrangements, for 72 puzzles across six editions. The daily puzzle draws from a separate set of 365 arrangements.

What does par mean?

Par is the shortest solution from the starting picture. You can take as many moves as you like. Use the photo button to see the original or ask for a hint from your current position.
