Privacy

Star Routes stores your settings and puzzle progress on your device. The game plays offline. This edition has no account, advertising SDK, tracking or analytics service.

Sharing

If you tap Share, iOS opens its share sheet with your puzzle result. You choose the destination. That destination handles what you send under its own terms.

Support and deletion

Optional support uses GitHub. Its privacy policy applies when you leave this site. Deleting the game from your device removes its local save. For a privacy question, visit game support.
