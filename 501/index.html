      How to play

      You start on 501 with 5 darts. Each dart is one answer from today's category, and its number comes off your score. Big names take big chunks. Niche ones are how you finish.

      Land on exactly 0 for a perfect game. If an answer would take you below 0, it's a bust: the dart is gone and your score stays put.

      Whatever's left after 5 darts is your score. Lowest wins, so send it to your friends. New board every day.
